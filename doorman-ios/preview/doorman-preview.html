Doorman

A smart bouncer for your phone. Tap through the app below.

Jump to a screen

You open TikTok. The door is closed.

Instead of TikTok, you see Doorman's block screen. It shows one line about your day: your next meeting and how many times you opened apps today.

Try it: tap “Ask Doorman” on the phone.

Time of day

Daytime: real reasons get a short pass. "Just checking" gets 1 minute.

What makes it special

Not a ChatGPT wrapperIt lives inside iOS Screen Time. A chatbot can't lock your apps.
AI on your iPhoneApple's on-device AI decides. Built-in rules take over on older phones.
PrivateNo server, no account. Your data stays on your phone.
Locks itself againWhen the pass ends, the app closes automatically.

See it on your real iPhone

  1. Download the doorman-ios folder from GitHub (link below).
  2. On your Mac, open Doorman.xcodeproj in Xcode.
  3. Pick your Apple ID as the Team (Signing & Capabilities).
  4. Plug in your iPhone and press ▶ Run.
